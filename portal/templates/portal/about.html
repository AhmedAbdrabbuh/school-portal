About Al Madina School

Dedicated to Islamic education and community excellence

Who We Are

Al Madina School is an Islamic educational institution committed to providing students with a holistic education rooted in the teachings of the Quran and the Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). We believe that knowledge is a trust from Allah, and we take that responsibility seriously.

Founded with the vision of serving our community, we strive to nurture the minds, hearts, and character of every student who walks through our doors. Our dedicated teachers work tirelessly to ensure every child reaches their full potential — academically, spiritually, and morally.

Our Core Values
  • Taqwa (God-consciousness) in all affairs
  • Excellence in Quranic and Islamic studies
  • Respect for students, parents, and educators
  • Community service and brotherhood/sisterhood
  • Continuous learning and self-improvement

Our Programs

  • Quran Memorization (Hifz)
    Full-time Quran memorization program
  • Tajweed Classes
    Proper Quranic recitation rules
  • Islamic Studies
    Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, and Aqeedah
  • Arabic Language
    Reading, writing, and conversation
